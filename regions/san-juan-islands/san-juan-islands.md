# San Juan Islands Dive Planning

## The region

The San Juan Islands are an archipelago of more than 400 islands and rocks above high water in the northwest corner of Washington and part of the Salish Sea, the inland sea shared by Washington and British Columbia, sitting where the Strait of Juan de Fuca to the south meets the Strait of Georgia to the north. Haro Strait bounds them on the west along the international boundary with Canada, and Rosario Strait on the east, between the islands and Fidalgo Island. Orcas Island (148 km²) and San Juan Island (143 km²) are the largest, followed by Lopez. Haro Strait carries most of the tidal exchange between the two straits and is the deepest water here, over 400 m just north of Turn Point on Stuart Island; Rosario Strait runs about 9 km wide at its southern entrance, between Lopez and Fidalgo Islands, with a main channel around 70 m deep.

| Reach | Where |
|---|---|
| **Haro Strait shore** | The west side of San Juan Island from its southwest corner north to Mosquito Pass, with Henry, Stuart and Spieden Islands beyond |
| **Strait of Juan de Fuca shore** | The south shores of San Juan Island west of Cattle Point and of Lopez Island, open to the strait |
| **Interior channels** | San Juan, Upright, Spieden and President Channels, and the passes between the islands: Cattle Pass at the south entrance of San Juan Channel, Mosquito, Pole, Wasp, Harney, Thatcher, Obstruction and Peavine |
| **Rosario Strait** | The east shores of Lopez, Decatur, Blakely and Orcas, the islands of Cypress, Sinclair and Guemes, and the west shore of Fidalgo Island from Anacortes through Burrows Bay to the western entrance of Deception Pass |
| **Northern islands** | Waldron, Sucia, Patos and Matia, along Boundary Pass and the Strait of Georgia; boat access only |

### Boundaries

- **This folder covers.** The whole San Juan archipelago, Guemes Island included, the channels and passes between the islands, Haro Strait out to the international boundary, Rosario Strait, and the Rosario Strait shore of Fidalgo Island from Anacortes and Guemes Channel south to the western entrance of Deception Pass.
- **The south shores of San Juan and Lopez.** They face the Strait of Juan de Fuca and take its swell, and are dived as island sites, off the island stations and the island logistics.
- **Southern edge on Fidalgo.** The Rosario Strait shore, ending at the western entrance of Deception Pass.

### The passes and the unequal tide

The islands split the exchange between two straits into a web of channels, and the water accelerates through every constriction. On a large spring ebb, Cattle Pass and Spieden Channel peak around 2.4 m/s and Rosario Strait around 1.8 m/s. The tide here is strongly mixed: the two daily exchanges are very unequal, so the same channel can run at a fraction of that speed on the weaker exchange of the same day, and on some days the weak exchange barely reverses at all, leaving a long, slow period with no clean slack. The weak exchange gives the long, easy windows; the slacks either side of a big ebb are short and turn hard. Upwellings, downwellings and whirlpools form off points and where channels meet, and the surface set off a point can run opposite to the set at depth.

### Shipping and ferries

Haro Strait and Boundary Pass carry deep-sea traffic to and from Vancouver in a traffic separation scheme, and Rosario Strait is a Vessel Traffic Service special area carrying tankers to the refineries at Anacortes and Cherry Point. The Cooperative Vessel Traffic Service, run jointly by the US and Canadian Coast Guards, covers all of it. Large ships in Haro Strait raise short-lived rollers on the shore well after they pass. Washington State Ferries run from Anacortes through Thatcher Pass, Harney Channel and Wasp Passage to Friday Harbor, Orcas, Lopez and Shaw, and their wakes reach the shore along the route. A boat dive near Haro or Rosario Strait or the ferry route needs a lookout and a dive flag that traffic can see.

## Conditions

- **Water temperature.** Roughly 7 to 12 °C through the year. Strong tidal mixing keeps the water column close to one temperature from top to bottom, so the surface warms only a little in summer. Drysuit year round.
- **Visibility.** Roughly 3 to 18 m, best on the outer shores facing the deep straits and worst in Rosario Strait and along the southern Fidalgo shore, where silty runoff holds it nearer 3 to 6 m. Plankton blooms in mid spring and again in mid to late summer can close the top several metres while the water below stays clear, and the Fraser River freshet spreads a brackish surface layer down Haro Strait from late spring to August. The flood generally brings clearer water than the ebb. Autumn and winter tend to run clearest, with the weather the trade-off. Recent reports beat any prediction.
- **Character.** Cold, clear, high-current diving among islands at the junction of three open straits: rock walls, ledges and kelp swept by strong tidal flow, with dense invertebrate cover. Tidal current decides almost every dive, and the shores open to the Strait of Juan de Fuca and the southern Haro Strait shore add long-fetch wind and swell. Shore sites on San Juan, Orcas, Lopez and Shaw are reached by Washington State Ferries from Anacortes, the northern islands by boat, and help is a ferry or air ambulance away.

| [Fidalgo Head](sites/fidalgo-head.md) | Rocky headland on the south side of Washington Park's bay at the west end of Fidalgo Island, reached by a fifteen minute compass swim across a muddy bay, its rock and kelp crowded with crabs, sea stars, urchins, chitons and nudibranchs. |
| [Green Point](sites/green-point.md) | Low basalt point at the northwest corner of Washington Park, Anacortes, followed along a kelp and eelgrass shoreline and rounded to a drop toward the channel, a strong nudibranch site with octopus dens in the rock at the point. |
| [Lime Kiln Point](sites/lime-kiln-point.md) | Steep wall below the Lime Kiln lighthouse in the Whale Watch Park on San Juan Island's Haro Strait shore, dropping from a bull kelp canopy over ledges, small walls and cascading boulders crowded with pink hydrocoral, sponges, scallops and anemones, with wolf eels and giant Pacific octopus in its dens and orcas passing close to the point. |

| [Odlin County Park (North)](sites/odlin-county-park-north.md) | Rocky shoreline at the north end of Odlin County Park on Lopez Island, followed north from the park's small fishing pier past boulders and crevices onto a steep cobble slope below Upright Head, with schools of small fish and pipefish under the pier and wolf eels, giant Pacific octopus and rockfish in the dens. |
| [Odlin County Park (South)](sites/odlin-county-park-south.md) | Sandy bay fronting Odlin County Park on Lopez Island, a swim from the beach across a shallow flat and eelgrass onto an open sand and mud slope facing Upright Channel, home to tube-dwelling anemones, orange sea pens and sturgeon poachers, with a kelp-fringed rocky shoreline along the bay's south side. |
| [Reuben Tarte County Park](sites/reuben-tarte.md) | Pebble cove on the northeast corner of San Juan Island where three channels meet, with rock walls, small canyons and cascading boulders on both sides of a rocky knoll, a lion-shaped boulder at the cove mouth, a soft nudibranch slope to the northwest, and rock crowded with sponges, Christmas anemones, red urchins, copper rockfish and lingcod. |
| [Rosario Beach](sites/rosario-beach.md) | Sanctuary bay in Deception Pass State Park on Rosario Strait with four dives from one beach (Urchin Rocks under bull kelp, Rosario Head into Sharpe Cove, the north cliffs, and the walls of Northwest Island), its rock thick with urchins, spider crabs and clouds of shrimp. |
| [Smallpox Bay (San Juan County Park)](sites/smallpox-bay.md) | Small sandy cove and boat ramp at San Juan County Park on the west side of San Juan Island, a campground shore dive around either rocky point onto ledges, boulders and undercuts under understory and bull kelp, crowded with cup corals, sponges, sea stars and lingcod, with orcas passing in Haro Strait. |
| [South Beach (American Camp)](sites/south-beach.md) | Long sand and cobble beach in the American Camp unit of San Juan Island National Historical Park, facing the Strait of Juan de Fuca toward the Olympic Mountains, a gentle slope through wide eelgrass bands onto a flat sand shelf with Cattle Point lighthouse in view, a small-life dive where sand lances erupt from the bottom in summer among gunnels, hooded nudibranchs and flatfish. |
| [Spencer Spit State Park (Frost Island)](sites/spencer-spit.md) | Colorful rock wall along the west side of Frost Island, across a narrow sandy channel from the tip of Spencer Spit on Lopez Island, its ledges and cracks crowded with burrowing sea cucumbers, red tunicates, cup corals, sea stars and stalked vase sponges, home to octopus, grunt sculpins, warbonnets and painted greenlings. |

## Dive shops and air fills

The islands have no public air fill. The dive locker at the University of Washington's Friday Harbor Labs fills air and nitrox for its own scientific divers only. Every cylinder for an island dive is filled on the mainland and carried over on the ferry, with spares for the whole trip.

- **Anacortes Diving and Supply, Anacortes.** 2502 Commercial Ave, Anacortes, WA 98221. (360) 293-2070. anacortesdiving.com. Open 9 am to 6 pm, seven days a week. Air and nitrox to about 240 bar, banked EAN32 and EAN40, plus argon; a nitrox cylinder needs a current nitrox band and sticker. A few minutes from the ferry terminal.

## Emergency

- **Local emergency.** 911. Call EMS first, then DAN once the diver is stabilized and transport is underway. On the islands the first stop is PeaceHealth Peace Island Medical Center in Friday Harbor (1117 Spring St, 24/7 emergency room); on Fidalgo it is Island Health in Anacortes (Commercial Ave and 26th St, (360) 299-1300, 24/7 emergency department). Island Health's hyperbaric unit treats wounds on weekdays only and is not a dive emergency chamber.
- **[DAN (Divers Alert Network)](https://dan.org).** +1-919-684-9111, 24/7, collect calls accepted worldwide. Its Annual Diving Report and Incident Insights are the standing source for dive medicine, gear, procedure and debrief questions.
- **Virginia Mason Franciscan Health, Center for Hyperbaric Medicine, Seattle.** 1100 9th Ave, Seattle, WA 98101. (206) 583-6543; emergency referrals from a physician or emergency department go through VMFH Mission Control at (206) 341-1141, 24/7. The only triple-lock multiplace recompression chamber in Western Washington and the region's chamber. About 1.5 hours by road from Anacortes; from the islands, a ferry crossing of 45 minutes to well over an hour on a sailing schedule comes first, so a serious case leaves by air ambulance.
