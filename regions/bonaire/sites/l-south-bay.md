# L. South Bay

A boat dive in the middle of Klein Bonaire's south shore, in the lee of the islet from the trade winds. A shallow sand and coral terrace runs about 100 m out from the beach to a reef that slopes away through large coral formations to about 30 m. Groupers hold on the reef, and schools of bar and horse-eye jacks hunt the schools of boga just off it. The calm water and gradual profile suit a first boat dive; the headline hazard is a wind or swell from the south, which runs straight onto this shore.

## Getting there

* **Entry.** A giant stride or back roll from a dive operator's boat on the mooring, then a descent onto the terrace.
* **Parking.** Not applicable.
* **Access.** Reached only by a dive operator's boat, about 15 minutes from Kralendijk. Klein Bonaire is open to visitors from 07:00 to 19:00. A valid STINAPA nature fee is required.
* **Facilities.** A STINAPA mooring over the terrace.

## Navigation and landmarks

* **The terrace.** Sand and coral at about 4 to 10 m, running about 100 m out from the beach, shallowest close to shore and deepening steadily toward its outer edge. It serves as the safety stop at the end of the dive, and as a snorkel site.
* **The reef.** Begins at the outer edge of the terrace at about 10 m and slopes away through large coral formations to about 30 m.

* **Drivers.** The east to southeast trade winds. The current can sit still for days, then run when the trades strengthen or shift.
* **Character.** Mild to none on most days, sheltered by the islet from the trades.

## Wind

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dive area shore facing** | south, 181° |

* **Bad.** South-southeast through west-southwest (SSE-WSW), onshore over more than 30 km of open water, raising waves at the mooring and surge over the terrace.
* **Short fetch.** Southeast (SE), onshore over 4.5 km from the southern half of Bonaire, enough to build a chop at the mooring.
* **Fine.** West clockwise through east-southeast: offshore from northwest through east-northeast, blowing off the islet itself, and cross-shore over 290 to 790 m from the west, west-northwest and east, and 4.6 km from the east-southeast.

## Visibility

* **Overall.** Up to about 30 m, at its best on calm mornings. A swell from the south-southeast through west-southwest stirs the terrace and lowers it in the shallows.

## Temperature

* **At depth.** 26 to 29 °C year round, coolest around February and March, warmest around August through October. The water holds one temperature from the surface to recreational depth. A wetsuit or dive skin is standard.

## Marine life

* **Off the reef.** Schools of boga hang in the water just off the reef, hunted by schools of bar and horse-eye jacks.
* **Reef.** Several species of grouper, with snapper, parrotfish and angelfish among the coral formations.

## Hazards

* **Wind and swell from the south.** A wind or swell from the south-southeast through west-southwest runs straight onto this shore over open water, putting waves at the mooring and surge over the shallow terrace.
* **The slope.** The reef gains depth steadily from the edge of the terrace at about 10 m to about 30 m; watch the computer along the slope.
* **Current.** Mild to none on most days, building when the trades strengthen; check the surface before entering.
* **Boat traffic.** Dive boats, water taxis and pleasure craft work the water off Klein Bonaire; a surface marker buoy marks a diver surfacing away from the mooring.
