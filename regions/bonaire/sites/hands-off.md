# M. Hands Off

Originally established to gauge how much impact divers themselves have on a reef, with no photographers or clumsy diving practices allowed when the site was set up; it remains a protected area with particularly fragile coral. The seabed here is unique for its many small hills and valleys, tongue and groove formations in diving terms, with a drop-off reaching about 37 m, cleaning stations and dense coral cover.

## Getting there

* **Entry.** By boat only.
* **Parking.** Not applicable.
* **Access.** Reached only by a dive operator's boat; Klein Bonaire itself has no facilities. Buoyancy control matters more here than at most Klein Bonaire sites, given the protected, fragile coral.
* **Facilities.** None.

## Navigation and landmarks

* **Tongue and groove formations.** Small hills and valleys across the seabed, the site's defining terrain feature.
* **The drop-off.** Reaches about 37 m.

## Current

No tidal current here; Bonaire's currents are wind driven.

## Depth

Depth is a plain observed figure, Bonaire has no tidal range worth normalizing against. The reef runs through the recreational range with a drop-off reaching about 37 m.

## Hazards

* **Fragile, protected coral.** Buoyancy discipline matters more here than usual; the site was established specifically to limit diver contact with the reef.

## Wind

* **Orientation.** Faces the west side of Klein Bonaire.
* **Bad.** A strong wind from the exposed quadrant raises chop.
* **Fine.** Calmer conditions, or wind blocked by the bulk of the islet.
* **Wind against current.** Worth checking before entry, as at any boat-only Klein Bonaire site.

## Visibility

Klein Bonaire's visibility runs excellent, commonly reaching up to 30 m or more.

## Temperature

* **At depth.** 26 to 29 C year round, coolest around February and March, warmest around August through October. No thermocline worth planning around at recreational depths.
* **Surface layer.** No sharp difference from depth here.

## Marine life

* **Reef.** Cleaning stations and dense, beautiful coral formations, kept especially healthy by the site's protected status.
