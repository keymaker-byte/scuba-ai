# 33. Town Pier

One of the most photographed dive sites in the world, and the one site on Bonaire that needs advance permission. Diving Town Pier needs the Harbourmaster's written permission, submitted with a copy of the diver's passport at least 24 hours ahead, and every group must be led by a local divemaster, a maximum of four divers each. Dives are almost always run at night with an island dive operator. The steel pilings carry decades of growth, the greatest variety of sponge size and shape found anywhere on Bonaire, and the site is a macro photographer's dream, with species rarely seen elsewhere on the island.

## Getting there

* **Entry.** Only with an island dive operator, almost always at night.
* **Parking.** Arranged through the dive operator.
* **Access.** Requires written permission from the Harbourmaster, submitted with a passport copy at least 24 hours in advance, and a local divemaster for every group of up to four divers, at a cost around $35 per person, not included in standard dive packages. Confirm evening shipping schedules before the dive and only go in calm weather, given the working harbour setting.
* **Facilities.** None at the site.

## Navigation and landmarks

* **The pilings.** The steel pilings supporting the pier are the entire dive, carrying decades of sponge growth.
* **Depth profile.** The dive stays shallow throughout, from about 3 to 10 m among the pilings.

* **Drivers.** The east to southeast trade winds. The current can sit still for days, then run hard when the trades pick up or shift.
* **Character.** The harbour setting is generally sheltered.

## Wind

| | |
|---|---|
| **Entry shore facing** | west, 261° |
| **Dive area shore facing** | west-southwest, 256° |

* **Bad.** Southwest through west-southwest (SW-WSW), onshore over more than 30 km of open water through the gap south of Klein Bonaire, and northwest (NW), onshore over 10.6 km. Any wind from these quarters raises swell under the pier.
* **Short fetch.** South-southwest (SSW), onshore over 3.7 km, and west through west-northwest (W-WNW), onshore over 1.7 to 1.8 km from Klein Bonaire, each enough to build a chop at the entry.
* **Fine.** North-northwest clockwise through south: offshore from north-northeast through southeast, off the land, and cross-shore over 340 m to 1.8 km from the north-northwest, north, south-southeast and south. Harbour traffic, more than the wind, sets the surface on most days.

## Visibility

* **Overall.** Typically excellent, 15 to 30 m, aided at night by the calm, sheltered harbour water.

## Temperature

* **At depth.** 26 to 29 C year round, coolest around February and March, warmest around August through October. No thermocline worth planning around at these shallow depths. A wetsuit or dive skin is standard.

## Marine life

* **The pilings.** The greatest variety of sponge size and shape found anywhere on Bonaire, with hundreds of macro photo subjects and species rarely seen at other sites.

## Hazards

* **Working harbour.** Check for scheduled evening shipping before diving, and only dive in calm weather.
* **Access requirements.** Harbourmaster paperwork and a local guide are mandatory; secure both before planning the dive.
