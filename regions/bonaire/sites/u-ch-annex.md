# U. Carl's Hill Annex (Yellowman's Reef)

A boat dive on the west side of Klein Bonaire toward its northwest tip, marked site U of Bonaire National Marine Park, also listed as C.H. Annex and Yellow Man. A sand and coral terrace at about 6 to 11 m runs out about 170 m from the shore before the reef drops away, facing west-northwest into the open Caribbean. The site is closed to diving; confirm with STINAPA that the closure has been lifted before planning a dive here.

## Getting there

* **Entry.** From a dive operator's boat, once the site reopens.
* **Parking.** Not applicable.
* **Access.** Closed to diving. Confirm status with STINAPA before planning a dive here. When open, the site is reached only by boat from Kralendijk; Klein Bonaire is open to visitors from 07:00 to 19:00, and a valid STINAPA nature fee is required.
* **Facilities.** Not established.

## Navigation and landmarks

* **The terrace.** Sand and coral shoaling to about 6 m at the shore, then 9 to 10 m for most of its width, running about 170 m out from the shore. The coordinate sits on it, about 100 m off the shore, where the bottom rises and dips between 7 and 11 m.
* **The edge.** About 70 m west of the coordinate the terrace ends and the reef drops steeply, past recreational depth within a few tens of metres.

## Wind

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dive area shore facing** | west-northwest, 285° |

* **Bad.** Southwest through north-northwest (SW-NNW), onshore over 6.6 to more than 30 km of open water, raising waves at the surface and surge over the terrace.
* **Mixed.** North (N), cross-shore over 5.3 km, running a swell along the reef.
* **Fine.** North-northeast clockwise through south-southwest: offshore from the northeast through the south-southeast, blowing off the islet itself over 100 to 180 m, and cross-shore over 4.4 km from the north-northeast and over 220 to 840 m from the south and south-southwest.

## Visibility

* **Overall.** Commonly 15 to 30 m or better along Klein Bonaire's west side, at its best on calm mornings. A wind from the southwest through the north-northwest stirs the terrace and lowers it in the shallows.

## Temperature

* **At depth.** 26 to 29 °C year round, coolest around February and March, warmest around August through October. The water holds one temperature from the surface to recreational depth. A wetsuit or dive skin is standard.

## Marine life

* **Overall.** Not established.

## Hazards

* **Closed site.** Dive here only once STINAPA confirms the closure has been lifted.
* **Wind and swell.** The site faces the open sea; a wind from the southwest through the north-northwest runs straight onto it, putting chop on the surface and surge over the terrace.
* **The drop.** The reef falls steeply from the outer edge of the terrace past recreational depth; watch the computer and the no-decompression limit on the way down.
* **Current.** Wind driven current can build when the trades strengthen or shift, making the return to the boat hard work.
* **Boat traffic.** Dive boats, water taxis and pleasure craft work the water off Klein Bonaire; a surface marker buoy marks a diver surfacing away from the boat.
