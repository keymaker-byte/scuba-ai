# 56. Red Beryl

A versatile shore dive about 11 km south of Kralendijk, marked by a row of yellow buoys next to Atlantis Beach, the island's main kitesurfing spot. The profile runs in three clear stages: a shallow sandy plateau to about 8 m good for a buoyancy check, a middle zone from 8 to 20 m where hard and soft coral thicken with cleaning stations, and a steep wall beyond 20 m running well past recreational range. Dive before 10:00 or after 16:00, and use extra caution on windy days, to stay clear of the kitesurfers who work the beach beside the site.

## Getting there

* **Entry.** A shore entry marked by a row of yellow buoys.
* **Parking.** Roadside at the site.
* **Access.** Open, but dive before 10:00 or after 16:00 and use extra caution on windy days to avoid the kitesurfers at neighbouring Atlantis Beach.
* **Facilities.** None at the site.

## Navigation and landmarks

* **Sandy plateau.** The shallow zone to about 8 m, good for a buoyancy check before heading out.
* **The wall.** Begins around 20 m and runs well past recreational limits.
* **Depth profile.** The sandy plateau sits to about 8 m, a coral transition zone runs from 8 to 20 m, and the wall begins beyond that.

* **Drivers.** The east to southeast trade winds. The current can sit still for days, then run hard when the trades pick up or shift.

## Wind

| | |
|---|---|
| **Entry shore facing** | southwest, 235° |
| **Dive area shore facing** | southwest, 233° |

* **Bad.** South through west-northwest (S-WNW), onshore over more than 30 km of open water. Any wind from this quarter raises swell at the entry.
* **Mixed.** South-southeast (SSE), cross-shore over more than 30 km, running a swell along the shore past the entry.
* **Fine.** Northwest clockwise through southeast: offshore from north through east-southeast, off the land, and cross-shore over 270 m to 1.4 km from the northwest, north-northwest and southeast. The prevailing east trades blow off the land here and leave the entry flat, but a wind strong enough for kitesurfing brings kite traffic from the neighbouring kite beach onto the surface; surface with extra caution on a windy day.

## Visibility

* **Overall.** Typically 15 to 30 m.

## Temperature

* **At depth.** 26 to 29 C year round, coolest around February and March, warmest around August through October. No thermocline worth planning around at recreational depths. A wetsuit or dive skin is standard.

## Marine life

* **Overall.** Barracuda, rays, sea turtles, scorpionfish, boxfish, pufferfish, surgeonfish and moray eels.
* **Middle zone.** Cleaning stations at the transition between coral communities.

## Hazards

* **Kitesurfers.** Active at the neighbouring beach; dive before 10:00 or after 16:00 and watch for kite lines on the surface, especially on a windy day.
