# 19. Kalli's Reef

A popular stretch of reef on Bonaire's northwest coast north of Kralendijk, named for Kalli De Meyer, manager of Bonaire National Marine Park from 1991. A shallow terrace below the rocky shore slopes to a reef crest at about 9 to 10 m, with extensive coral formations from there to about 18 m and the reef running on deeper. The site is dived from a boat, and its shallows also make an excellent snorkel from the boat. Seahorses and turtles are regular. The headline hazard is wind driven current, which can sit still for days and then run when the trades strengthen or shift.

## Getting there

* **Entry.** A giant stride from a boat tied to the site mooring, which sits close inshore, then a short descent to the reef crest.
* **Parking.** None; the site is dived from a boat.
* **Access.** By boat from Kralendijk. The shore here is a rocky ledge with a large step down to the water and a hard climb back out with gear, too risky for a shore entry. Open to diving; confirm status with STINAPA before planning a dive. The Nature Fee e-tag is required.
* **Facilities.** None.

## Navigation and landmarks

* **Shore terrace.** A shallow terrace at about 4 to 5 m runs along the foot of the rocky shore.
* **Slope to the crest.** From the terrace the bottom slopes southwest, reaching about 7 m some 60 m out and about 9 m at the dive area coordinate.
* **Reef crest.** The crest sits at about 9 to 10 m, roughly 90 to 130 m off the shore, and holds the shallow end of the extensive coral formations.
* **Coral formations.** They run from the crest down to about 18 m, the core of the dive, and the reef continues deeper toward 30 m.

* **Drivers.** The east to southeast trade winds. The current can sit still for days, then run hard when the trades pick up or shift.

## Wind

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dive area shore facing** | southwest, 236° |

* **Bad.** South-southwest through west-northwest (SSW-WNW), onshore over 5.5 km to more than 30 km of open sea. Any wind from this quarter raises a sea at the mooring and on the reef top.
* **Short fetch.** South (S), onshore over 3.7 km from Klein Bonaire, enough to build a chop on the surface.
* **Mixed.** South-southeast (SSE), cross-shore over 8.2 km down the coast, running a swell along the shore.
* **Fine.** Northwest clockwise through southeast: offshore from north through east-southeast, off the land, and cross-shore over 190 m to 2.2 km from the northwest, north-northwest and southeast.

## Visibility

* **Overall.** Typically excellent, 15 to 30 m and sometimes more, best on calm mornings.
* **Wind and swell.** A westerly blow or a northern swell stirs the shallow terrace and can haze the water near the crest.

## Temperature

* **At depth.** 26 to 29 °C year round, coolest around February and March, warmest around August through October. The temperature holds steady through recreational depths. A wetsuit or dive skin is standard.

## Marine life

* **Seahorses.** Regular among the coral formations.
* **Turtles.** Green turtles are seen often on the reef.
* **Reef fish.** Parrotfish, angelfish, trumpetfish, pufferfish and boxfish across the reef, with moray eels in its holes.
* **Larger fish.** Barracuda along the reef edge, and tarpon passing through.

## Hazards

* **Current.** Wind driven current builds when the trades strengthen; check the surface before entering.
* **Swell.** Northern swell in winter, and swell from passing tropical systems from June to November, reaches this shore under a light breeze and makes the boat entry and exit harder.
* **Shore ledge.** The rocky ledge along the shore is a poor emergency exit; plan to surface at the boat.
