# C. Jerry's Reef

Also known as Jerry's Sponges, an extensive sponge garden about 170 m from Ebo's Reef on Klein Bonaire's north side, with a deep water mooring requiring a swim to reach the reef. Tube and vase sponges of various sizes dominate, alongside crinoids, elephant ear sponges and fern-like black coral, and the site is a reliable spot for turtles, stingrays and spotted eagle rays.

## Getting there

* **Entry.** By boat only; the mooring sits in deep water, with a swim to reach the shallower reef.
* **Parking.** Not applicable.
* **Access.** Reached only by a dive operator's boat; Klein Bonaire itself has no facilities.
* **Facilities.** None.

## Navigation and landmarks

* **The deep mooring.** In deep water, a swim from the reef.
* **Sponge garden.** Tube and vase sponges of varied size, the site's central feature.
* **Depth profile.** The reef runs through the recreational range to about 30 m, with the mooring itself in much deeper water.

* **Drivers.** The east to southeast trade winds. The current can sit still for days, then run hard when the trades pick up or shift.

## Wind

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dive area shore facing** | northeast, 48° |

* **Short fetch.** North through northeast (N-NE), onshore over 1 to 2.1 km, and east-southeast (ESE), onshore over 1.7 km, across the channel from Bonaire, each enough to build a chop at the mooring. A strong trade raises chop over the open swim to the mooring.
* **Mixed.** Northwest (NW), cross-shore over 8.4 km, running a swell along the reef.
* **Fine.** East-northeast through east, southeast clockwise through west-northwest, and north-northwest: onshore over 850 to 910 m from the east-northeast and east, offshore from south through west-northwest, off the islet, and cross-shore over 2.8 to 4.6 km from the southeast, south-southeast and north-northwest.

## Visibility

* **Overall.** Klein Bonaire's visibility runs excellent, commonly reaching up to 40 m.

## Temperature

* **At depth.** 26 to 29 C year round, coolest around February and March, warmest around August through October. No thermocline worth planning around at recreational depths. A wetsuit or dive skin is standard.

## Marine life

* **Reef.** Turtles, stingrays and spotted eagle rays are all reliably seen, alongside crinoids, elephant ear sponges and fern-like black coral.

## Hazards

* **Open water swim.** The mooring sits well offshore in deep water; plan the swim to and from the reef accordingly.
