# 25. Small Wall

A vertical wall in front of the Black Durgon Inn, beginning around 6 m and leading down to a small cave at about 18 m that sometimes shelters a sleeping nurse shark or a resident green moray, with black brotulas nearby. There is no public shore access, so this is a boat dive. The surface here is among the calmest on Bonaire.

|---|---|

| **Coordinates** | 12.179567, -68.2938 (about 13 m) |

## Getting there

* **Entry.** By boat, at the mooring.
* **Parking.** Not applicable for the boat entry.
* **Access.** Boat only; there is no public shore access.
* **Facilities.** The Black Durgon Inn sits directly onshore.

## Navigation and landmarks

* **The wall.** Begins around 6 m.
* **The cave.** A small feature, about 3 to 4.5 m across, at the wall's base around 18 m.
* **Depth profile.** The wall runs from about 6 m to the cave at its base around 18 m.

## Current

| | |
|---|---|
| **Current type** | Wind driven |
| **Usual set** | Not established |
| **Typical speed** | Not established |

* **Drivers.** The east to southeast trade winds. The current can sit still for days, then run hard when the trades pick up or shift.

## Wind

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dive area shore facing** | west-southwest, 248° |

* **Bad.** West-southwest through northwest (WSW-NW), onshore over 5 km to more than 30 km of open water. Any wind from this quarter raises swell, though the site is sheltered enough to stay calm on most days regardless.
* **Short fetch.** South-southwest through southwest (SSW-SW), onshore over 1.6 to 1.8 km from Klein Bonaire, enough to build a chop on the surface.
* **Fine.** North-northwest clockwise through south: offshore from north-northeast through southeast, off the land, and cross-shore over 170 m to 2.2 km from the north-northwest, north, south-southeast and south.

## Visibility

* **Overall.** Typically excellent, 15 to 30 m.

## Temperature

* **At depth.** 26 to 29 C year round, coolest around February and March, warmest around August through October. No thermocline worth planning around at recreational depths. A wetsuit or dive skin is standard.

## Marine life

* **The cave.** A sleeping nurse shark or resident green moray turns up here, with black brotulas nearby.
* **Sandy bottom.** Large tarpon, eels and eagle rays.
* **Reef.** Resident groupers and prawns.

## Hazards

* **Current.** Wind driven current builds when the trades strengthen; check the surface before entering.
