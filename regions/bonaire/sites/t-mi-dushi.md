# T. Mi Dushi

Mi Dushi means my sweetheart in Papiamentu, a double reef site on Klein Bonaire's southwest side well suited to beginners and checkout dives. Staghorn and yellow pencil coral fill the shallows, home to smaller reef fish including yellow headed jawfish and sailfin blennies; the staghorn here has thinned along with the rest of Bonaire's in the bleaching events since 2023. This is currently one of the more reliable seahorse sites on the islet, with several regularly seen around 15 m, especially in the transition zone between the lagoon and the sand channel; a guide familiar with the site's reef zones is worth having on a first visit.

## Getting there

* **Entry.** By boat, at the mooring.
* **Parking.** Not applicable.
* **Access.** Reached only by a dive operator's boat; a guide is recommended for a first visit to find the seahorses reliably. Klein Bonaire itself has no facilities.
* **Facilities.** None.

## Navigation and landmarks

* **The transition zone.** Between the lagoon and the sand channel, the most reliable spot for seahorses.
* **Depth profile.** The shallows hold staghorn and yellow pencil coral, with seahorses typically found around 15 m.

* **Drivers.** The east to southeast trade winds. The current can sit still for days, then run hard when the trades pick up or shift.

## Wind

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dive area shore facing** | west-northwest, 300° |

* **Bad.** West-southwest through north (WSW-N), onshore over 5.7 km to more than 30 km of open water. Any wind from this quarter raises chop at the mooring.
* **Mixed.** Southwest (SW), cross-shore over more than 30 km, running a swell along the reef.
* **Fine.** North-northeast clockwise through south-southwest: offshore from east-northeast through south, off the islet, and cross-shore over 350 m to 4.8 km from the north-northeast, northeast and south-southwest.

## Visibility

* **Overall.** Klein Bonaire's visibility runs excellent, commonly reaching up to 30 m or more.

## Temperature

* **At depth.** 26 to 29 C year round, coolest around February and March, warmest around August through October. No thermocline worth planning around at recreational depths. A wetsuit or dive skin is standard.

## Marine life

* **Shallows.** Staghorn and yellow pencil coral, thinned since 2023, home to yellow headed jawfish and sailfin blennies.
* **Transition zone.** Several seahorses regularly seen around 15 m.

## Hazards

* **Current.** Wind driven current builds when the trades strengthen; check the surface before entering.
