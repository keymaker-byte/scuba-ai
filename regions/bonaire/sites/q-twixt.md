# Q. Twixt

A boat dive on the west side of Klein Bonaire, facing open sea away from Bonaire. A sand and reef terrace at about 8 to 10 m runs out close to 200 m from the shore, and past its seaward edge the bottom falls away steeply, passing 40 m within about 100 m of the edge and 60 m within about 150 m. The headline hazards are the steep drop past the terrace edge and its exposure to any wind from the southwest round to the north-northwest.

## Getting there

* **Entry.** From a dive operator's boat tied to a STINAPA mooring, since anchoring is banned throughout the marine park.
* **Parking.** Not applicable.
* **Access.** Reached only by a dive operator's boat. Klein Bonaire is open to visitors from 07:00 to 19:00, and a valid STINAPA nature fee is required. Confirm the site is open, and its mooring in place, with STINAPA or the operator before a trip.
* **Facilities.** None.

## Navigation and landmarks

* **The terrace.** Sand and reef at about 8 to 10 m, running out close to 200 m from the shore, with the shallows inshore of it rising to about 5 m some 80 m from the shore.
* **The drop.** Past the seaward edge of the terrace, about 40 m beyond the coordinate, the bottom falls steeply, passing 40 m within about 100 m of the edge and 60 m within about 150 m.

* **Drivers.** The east to southeast trade winds. The current can sit still for days, then run hard when the trades pick up or shift.

## Wind

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dive area shore facing** | west-northwest, 288° |

* **Bad.** Southwest through north-northwest (SW-NNW), onshore over 8 to more than 30 km of open water, raising waves at the mooring and surge over the terrace.
* **Mixed.** North through north-northeast (N-NNE), cross-shore over 5.7 to 6.6 km, and south through south-southwest (S-SSW), cross-shore over more than 30 km of open water, each running a swell along the reef.
* **Fine.** Northeast clockwise through south-southeast: offshore, blowing off the islet itself over 160 to 610 m.

## Visibility

* **Overall.** Commonly 15 to 30 m or better, at its best on calm mornings. A wind from the southwest through north-northwest, or a northern swell in winter, stirs the terrace and lowers it in the shallows.

## Temperature

* **At depth.** 26 to 29 °C year round, coolest around February and March, warmest around August through October. The water holds one temperature from the surface to recreational depth. A wetsuit or dive skin is standard.

## Marine life

* **Overall.** Not established.

## Hazards

* **The drop.** The bottom falls steeply past the terrace edge and passes 40 m within about 100 m of it; watch the computer and the no-decompression limit once off the terrace.
* **Current.** Wind driven current builds when the trades strengthen or shift and can make the return to the mooring hard work; check the surface before entering.
* **Wind and swell.** A wind from the southwest through north-northwest runs onto this shore over open water and puts chop at the mooring and surge over the terrace. Northern swell in winter, and swell from passing tropical systems from June to November, reach this side of the islet under a light breeze.
* **Boat traffic.** Dive boats and pleasure craft work the water off Klein Bonaire; a surface marker buoy marks a diver surfacing away from the mooring.
* **Status.** Confirm the site is open before planning a dive here.
