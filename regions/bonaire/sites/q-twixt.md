# Q. Twixt

A boat dive on Klein Bonaire's west side: two reefs separated by sand, a challenging dive with sightings of Caribbean reef sharks and barracuda.

## Getting there

* **Entry.** By boat only.
* **Parking.** Not applicable.
* **Access.** Reached only by a dive operator's boat; check with a dive shop that it is part of their current rotation. Klein Bonaire itself has no facilities.
* **Facilities.** None.

## Navigation and landmarks

* **Two reefs separated by sand.** The site's basic structure.

* **Drivers.** The east to southeast trade winds. The current can sit still for days, then run hard when the trades pick up or shift.
* **Character.** Current runs stronger here than at most Klein Bonaire sites.

## Wind

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dive area shore facing** | west-northwest, 288° |

* **Bad.** Southwest through north-northwest (SW-NNW), onshore over 8.1 km to more than 30 km of open water. Any wind from this quarter raises chop at the mooring.
* **Mixed.** North through north-northeast (N-NNE), cross-shore over 5.7 to 6.6 km, and south through south-southwest (S-SSW), cross-shore over more than 30 km, each running a swell along the reef.
* **Fine.** Northeast clockwise through south-southeast: offshore, off the islet.

## Visibility

* **Overall.** Klein Bonaire's visibility runs excellent, commonly reaching up to 30 m or more.

## Temperature

* **At depth.** 26 to 29 C year round, coolest around February and March, warmest around August through October. No thermocline worth planning around at recreational depths. A wetsuit or dive skin is standard.

## Marine life

* **Reef.** Caribbean reef sharks and barracuda.

## Hazards

* **More demanding than most Klein Bonaire sites.** Go with a guide familiar with it.
