# 60. Red Slave

The southernmost dive site on Bonaire's leeward coast, next to the orange painted slave huts that memorialize the island's period of slavery. Moderate to strong current is normal here, and the site is recommended for experienced divers only. Offshore schools of baitfish, bigeye scad known locally as masbango, are a highlight, often visibly being hunted by larger predatory fish, alongside horse-eye jacks and turtles.

## Getting there

* **Entry.** A shore entry typical of the southern tip, or by boat.
* **Parking.** Roadside, near the orange slave huts.
* **Access.** Open.
* **Facilities.** None at the site.

## Navigation and landmarks

* **Orange slave huts.** Stand onshore next to the site, a historical memorial and the clearest landmark.
* **Depth profile.** The reef runs from about 5 m through the recreational range to 25 m.

## Current

* **Drivers.** The east to southeast trade winds. The current can sit still for days, then run hard when the trades pick up or shift.
* **Character.** Moderate to strong current is typical here.

## Wind

| | |
|---|---|
| **Entry shore facing** | southwest, 234° |
| **Dive area shore facing** | west-southwest, 238° |

* **Bad.** South through west-northwest (S-WNW), onshore over more than 30 km of open water. Any wind with south or west in it adds swell to a site already driven by strong current.
* **Mixed.** Southeast through south-southeast (SE-SSE), cross-shore over more than 30 km past the southern tip, running a swell along the shore past the entry.
* **Fine.** Northwest clockwise through east-southeast: offshore from north through east-southeast, off the land, and cross-shore over 410 m to 1.2 km from the northwest and north-northwest. The east trades blow off the land here and leave the entry flat.

## Visibility

* **Overall.** Typically 15 to 30 m.

## Temperature

* **At depth.** 26 to 29 C year round, coolest around February and March, warmest around August through October. No thermocline worth planning around at recreational depths. A wetsuit or dive skin is standard.

## Marine life

* **Open water.** Offshore schools of baitfish, bigeye scad (masbango in Papiamentu), often visibly hunted by larger predatory fish, along with horse-eye jacks.
* **Reef.** Turtles regularly seen.

## Hazards

* **Current.** Moderate to strong as a rule; suited to divers with prior current experience.
