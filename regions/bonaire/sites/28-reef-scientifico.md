# 28. Reef Scientifico

A healthy coral garden just south of La Machaca, named for a grid system once built on the reef to monitor algae growth, remains of which are still found while diving. The site is particularly good after dark, when free swimming morays and sleeping parrotfish are common.

## Getting there

* **Entry.** A straightforward shore entry off Captain Don's Habitat, by its pier and ladder.
* **Parking.** At Captain Don's Habitat.
* **Access.** Open.
* **Facilities.** Captain Don's Habitat resort sits onshore.

## Navigation and landmarks

* **Research grid remains.** Traces of the algae monitoring grid the site is named for are still visible on the reef.
* **Depth profile.** The reef sits around 13 m.

* **Drivers.** The east to southeast trade winds. The current can sit still for days, then run hard when the trades pick up or shift.

## Wind

| | |
|---|---|
| **Entry shore facing** | west-southwest, 242° |
| **Dive area shore facing** | west-southwest, 246° |

* **Bad.** South (S), onshore over 5.4 km down the coast, and west through west-northwest (W-WNW), onshore over more than 30 km of open water. Any wind from these quarters raises swell at the entry.
* **Short fetch.** South-southwest through west-southwest (SSW-WSW), onshore over 1.1 to 1.3 km from Klein Bonaire, enough to build a chop at the entry.
* **Mixed.** Northwest (NW), cross-shore over 6.6 km, running a swell along the shore past the entry.
* **Fine.** North-northwest clockwise through south-southeast: offshore from north through east-southeast, off the land, and cross-shore over 280 to 670 m from the north-northwest, southeast and south-southeast.

## Visibility

* **Overall.** Typically excellent, 15 to 30 m.

## Temperature

* **At depth.** 26 to 29 C year round, coolest around February and March, warmest around August through October. No thermocline worth planning around at recreational depths. A wetsuit or dive skin is standard.

## Marine life

* **Night.** Free swimming morays and sleeping parrotfish are common after dark, the site's particular strength.
* **Reef.** A healthy coral garden with a good range of typical west coast reef fish.

## Hazards

* **Current.** Wind driven current builds when the trades strengthen; check the surface before entering.
