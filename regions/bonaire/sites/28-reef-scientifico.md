# 28. Reef Scientifico

A healthy coral garden just south of La Machaca, named for a grid system once built on the reef to monitor algae growth, remains of which are still found while diving. Conditions match La Machaca, and the site is particularly good after dark, when free swimming morays and sleeping parrotfish are common.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Location** | West coast, Bonaire, off Captain Don's Habitat, just south of La Machaca |
| **Coordinates** | 12.172176, -68.2898 (dive area, about 7 m); entry at 12.172326, -68.289063, the same pier and ladder at Captain Don's Habitat used for La Machaca |
| **Type** | Shore |
| **Depth range** | Around 13 m |

## Getting there

* **Entry.** A straightforward shore entry off Captain Don's Habitat, the same access as La Machaca.
* **Parking.** At Captain Don's Habitat.
* **Access.** Open.
* **Facilities.** Captain Don's Habitat resort sits onshore.

## Navigation and landmarks

* **Research grid remains.** Traces of the algae monitoring grid the site is named for are still visible on the reef.
* **La Machaca.** Sits just north, sharing the same entry and similar conditions.

## Current

No tidal current here; Bonaire's currents are wind driven.

## Depth

Depth is a plain observed figure, Bonaire has no tidal range worth normalizing against. The reef sits around 13 m, similar to La Machaca just north of it.

## Hazards

None notable beyond the general reef and current awareness that applies along this coast.

## Wind

* **Orientation.** Faces west.
* **Bad.** Any wind with north or west in it raises swell.
* **Fine.** The prevailing east to southeast trade winds blow offshore to cross-shore.
* **Wind against current.** A building northerly against any current here is the combination to avoid.

## Visibility

West coast visibility here is typically excellent, in the same 15 to 30 m range as the rest of the leeward coast.

## Temperature

* **At depth.** 26 to 29 C year round, coolest around February and March, warmest around August through October. No thermocline worth planning around at recreational depths.
* **Surface layer.** No sharp difference from depth here.

## Marine life

* **Night.** Free swimming morays and sleeping parrotfish are common after dark, the site's particular strength.
* **Reef.** A healthy coral garden with a good range of typical west coast reef fish.
