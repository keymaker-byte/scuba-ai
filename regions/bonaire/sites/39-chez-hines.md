# 39. Chez Hines

Also known as South Belnem, a boat dive on the Belnem coast south of town. A shallow plateau with good coral and abundant marine life gives way to a second reef at about 37 m, below recreational range. Turtles are seen here more reliably than almost anywhere else on the island, alongside rays making their way along the coastline and nurse sharks resting in the shallows.

## Getting there

* **Entry.** By boat, at the mooring.
* **Parking.** Not applicable for the boat entry.
* **Access.** Boat only.
* **Facilities.** None at the site.

## Navigation and landmarks

* **Shallow plateau.** The main dive area, with good coral cover.
* **Second reef.** At about 37 m, beyond the EAN32 limit and the no-decompression range; the dive stays on the plateau and upper reef.
* **Depth profile.** The shallow plateau runs through the recreational range to about 30 m, with a second reef further out at about 37 m.

* **Drivers.** The east to southeast trade winds. The current can sit still for days, then run hard when the trades pick up or shift.
* **Character.** Current can run strong at times, more so than the sites immediately north.

## Wind

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dive area shore facing** | west-northwest, 300° |

* **Bad.** West-southwest through west-northwest (WSW-WNW), onshore over more than 30 km of open water. Any wind from this quarter raises swell on the plateau.
* **Short fetch.** Northwest through north (NW-N), onshore over 3.2 to 4.9 km across the bay off town, enough to build a chop on the surface.
* **Mixed.** South-southwest through southwest (SSW-SW), cross-shore over more than 30 km, running a swell along the shore.
* **Fine.** North-northeast clockwise through south: offshore from east-northeast through south, off the land, and cross-shore over 570 m to 4.2 km from the north-northeast and northeast.

## Visibility

* **Overall.** Typically excellent, 15 to 30 m.

## Temperature

* **At depth.** 26 to 29 C year round, coolest around February and March, warmest around August through October. No thermocline worth planning around at recreational depths. A wetsuit or dive skin is standard.

## Marine life

* **Shallow plateau.** Parrotfish, nurse sharks and trumpetfish, with turtles spotted here more than almost anywhere else on the island.
* **Along the coastline.** Rays making their way along the shallows.

## Hazards

* **Current.** Can run strong at times; check the surface before entering.
