# 17. Weber's Joy (Witch's Hut)

A double reef system on the protected coast south of 1000 Steps, officially named Weber's Joy on the marine park map and known locally as Witch's Hut after a small house that once stood on the beach. A short walk down a paved path to a rocky beach leads into an easy, gently sloping entry suited to any level, with rich fish life that makes it a favourite for wide-angle photography.

## Getting there

* **Entry.** A paved path leads from the road to a rocky beach that slopes gradually into the water.
* **Parking.** Directly across the street from the site.
* **Access.** Open.
* **Facilities.** None at the site.

## Navigation and landmarks

* **The mooring.** A large lavender stovepipe sponge sits just south of it.
* **Double reef.** The site's structure runs as two parallel reef systems.
* **Depth profile.** The reef begins around 3 m and slopes through the recreational range, with some routes reaching 40 m.

## Current

| | |
|---|---|
| **Current type** | Wind driven |
| **Usual set** | Not established |
| **Typical speed** | Not established |

* **Drivers.** The east to southeast trade winds. The current can sit still for days, then run hard when the trades pick up or shift.
* **Character.** Weber's Joy typically runs minimal current.

## Wind

| | |
|---|---|
| **Entry shore facing** | southwest, 215° |
| **Dive area shore facing** | southwest, 218° |

The swim out runs 139 m west-northwest from the entry, along the shore, to the dive area.

* **Bad.** South-southwest through west (SSW-W), onshore over more than 30 km of open sea. Any wind from this quarter raises swell at the entry.
* **Short fetch.** South-southeast through south (SSE-S), onshore over 4.6 to 4.8 km from the direction of Klein Bonaire and the town shore, enough to build a chop at the entry.
* **Fine.** West-northwest clockwise through southeast: offshore from north-northwest through east, off the land, and cross-shore over 160 m to 3.3 km from the west-northwest, northwest, east-southeast and southeast.

## Visibility

* **Overall.** Averages around 25 m, excellent even by the standards of Bonaire's leeward coast.

## Temperature

* **At depth.** 26 to 29 C year round, coolest around February and March, warmest around August through October. No thermocline worth planning around at recreational depths. A wetsuit or dive skin is standard.

## Marine life

* **Reef.** Rock beauties, angelfish and butterflyfish abound, along with schools of squid, parrotfish and soapfish among the sponges and corals.
* **Macro life.** Giant anemones, Pederson cleaner shrimp, corkscrew anemones, arrow crabs, octopus and nudibranchs.

## Hazards

* **Current.** Wind driven current builds when the trades strengthen; check the surface before entering.
