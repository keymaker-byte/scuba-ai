# D. Just a Nice Dive

A relaxed site on Klein Bonaire's east side with minimal current, less dramatic topography than some of the islet's wall dives, and correspondingly good for macro photography and night diving. Yellow pencil coral and leaf or sheet corals are abundant, schooling fish work the top of the reef, and solitary species such as ocean triggerfish turn up regularly.

## Getting there

* **Entry.** By boat only.
* **Parking.** Not applicable.
* **Access.** Reached only by a dive operator's boat; Klein Bonaire itself has no facilities.
* **Facilities.** None.

## Navigation and landmarks

* **The reef top.** Where the schooling fish and yellow pencil coral concentrate, the main working depth of the dive.
* **Depth profile.** The reef runs through the recreational range to about 30 m.

* **Drivers.** The east to southeast trade winds. The current can sit still for days, then run hard when the trades pick up or shift.
* **Character.** Minimal current is typical.

## Wind

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dive area shore facing** | southeast, 138° |

* **Bad.** South through south-southwest (S-SSW), onshore over more than 30 km of open water. Any wind from this quarter raises swell at the mooring.
* **Short fetch.** East through south-southeast (E-SSE), onshore over 2 to 2.6 km across the channel from Bonaire, enough to build a chop at the mooring; a strong easterly trade does exactly that on this side of the islet.
* **Mixed.** Southwest (SW), cross-shore over more than 30 km, running a swell along the reef.
* **Fine.** West-southwest clockwise through east-northeast: offshore from west through north-northeast, off the islet, and cross-shore over 440 m to 2 km from the west-southwest, northeast and east-northeast.

## Visibility

* **Overall.** Klein Bonaire's visibility runs excellent, commonly reaching up to 40 m.

## Temperature

* **At depth.** 26 to 29 C year round, coolest around February and March, warmest around August through October. No thermocline worth planning around at recreational depths. A wetsuit or dive skin is standard.

## Marine life

* **Reef top.** Schooling fish and abundant yellow pencil coral, leaf and sheet corals.
* **Elsewhere on the reef.** Solitary species such as ocean triggerfish, and many juvenile fish, good subjects for macro photography.

## Hazards

* **Current.** Wind driven current builds when the trades strengthen; check the surface before entering.
