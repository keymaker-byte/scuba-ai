# 41. Punt Vierkant

Dutch for square point, and the first of a run of double reef sites heading south, with two parallel coral walls separated by a sand channel. A flat, sandy entry makes it a popular choice for dive schools running certifications, and the inner reef slopes gradually from the shallows down to about 25 m.

## Getting there

* **Entry.** A flat, sandy shore entry, easy enough that dive schools use it for certification dives.
* **Parking.** Roadside at the site.
* **Access.** Open.
* **Facilities.** None at the site.

## Navigation and landmarks

* **The sand channel.** Separates the inner and outer reef walls, the defining structure of the double reef system that begins here.

## Current

No tidal current here; Bonaire's currents are wind driven.

## Depth

Depth is a plain observed figure, Bonaire has no tidal range worth normalizing against. The inner reef starts shallow at the sandy entry and slopes gradually to about 25 m.

## Hazards

None notable beyond the general reef and current awareness that applies along this coast.

## Wind

| | |
|---|---|
| **Entry shore facing** | southwest, 227° |
| **Dive area shore facing** | southwest, 229° |

The swim out runs 191 m south from the entry, along the shore, to the dive area.

* **Bad.** South through west-northwest (S-WNW), onshore over more than 30 km of open water. Any wind from this quarter raises swell at the entry.
* **Mixed.** Northwest (NW), cross-shore over more than 30 km, running a swell along the shore past the entry.
* **Fine.** North-northwest clockwise through south-southeast: offshore from north through east-southeast, off the land, and cross-shore over 320 m to 2.8 km from the north-northwest, southeast and south-southeast. The prevailing east to southeast trades blow off the land here and leave the entry flat.

## Visibility

West coast visibility here is typically excellent, in the same 15 to 30 m range as the rest of the leeward coast.

## Temperature

* **At depth.** 26 to 29 C year round, coolest around February and March, warmest around August through October. No thermocline worth planning around at recreational depths.
* **Surface layer.** No sharp difference from depth here.

## Marine life

* **Reef.** Schooling fish of many types, along with hard and soft coral and the sea turtles typical of this stretch of coast.
