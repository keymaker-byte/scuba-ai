# 18. Jeff Davis Memorial

An isolated dive and snorkel site off the coast road north of Kralendijk, named for Dr. Jefferson Davis, a NASA scientist regarded as the father of diving medicine for pioneering the use of hyperbaric oxygen to treat decompression sickness. Gorgonian-covered shallows lead to large coral accumulations that form chutes down to a sandy bottom, and the site's isolation makes for a string of cleaning stations where smaller fish tend larger visitors.

## Getting there

* **Entry.** Step down over rocks to reach the water, then a 100 m swim south-southwest to the dive area.
* **Parking.** Minimal, roadside at the yellow marker stone.
* **Access.** Open.
* **Facilities.** None at the site.

## Navigation and landmarks

* **Coral chutes.** Large coral accumulations form channels leading down to the sandy bottom.
* **Cleaning stations.** Scattered through the site, worth pausing at.
* **Depth profile.** The gorgonian-covered shallows sit around 6 m, with the coral chutes leading down through the recreational range to a sandy bottom.

* **Drivers.** The east to southeast trade winds. The current can sit still for days, then run hard when the trades pick up or shift.

## Wind

| | |
|---|---|
| **Entry shore facing** | southwest, 214° |
| **Dive area shore facing** | southwest, 214° |

* **Bad.** South-southwest through west (SSW-W), onshore over more than 30 km of open sea. Any wind from this quarter raises swell at the rock entry.
* **Short fetch.** South-southeast through south (SSE-S), onshore over 4.1 to 4.7 km from the direction of Klein Bonaire and the town shore, enough to build a chop on the surface.
* **Fine.** West-northwest clockwise through southeast: offshore from north-northwest through east, off the land, and cross-shore over 350 m to 4.1 km from the west-northwest, northwest, east-southeast and southeast.

## Visibility

* **Overall.** Typically excellent, 15 to 30 m.

## Temperature

* **At depth.** 26 to 29 C year round, coolest around February and March, warmest around August through October. No thermocline worth planning around at recreational depths. A wetsuit or dive skin is standard.

## Marine life

* **Shallows.** Dense gorgonian cover.
* **Reef.** Turtles and seahorses are regularly spotted, along with the fish working the site's cleaning stations.

## Hazards

* **Rock entry.** Rough footing over rock down to the water.
