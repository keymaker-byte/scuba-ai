# B. Ebo's Reef

One of the most recognizable dive sites on Klein Bonaire, named for Ebo Domacassé, one of Bonaire's first certified dive instructors. Giant orange elephant ear sponges, some over a metre across and often topped with black crinoids, scatter across the reef between about 5 and 20 m, alongside huge colonies of lavender stovepipe sponge that make excellent wide-angle photography subjects. The mooring barrel itself sits in deep water at about 43 m, with a short swim over blue water to reach the reef and again on the return.

## Getting there

* **Entry.** By boat only; a short swim over open blue water connects the deep mooring to the reef.
* **Parking.** Not applicable.
* **Access.** Reached only by a dive operator's boat; Klein Bonaire itself has no facilities.
* **Facilities.** None.

## Navigation and landmarks

* **The deep mooring.** Sits at about 43 m; the swim from here to the reef and back is part of the dive.
* **Elephant ear sponges.** Giant, orange, often crinoid-topped, scattered across the reef from about 5 to 20 m.

## Current

No tidal current here; Bonaire's currents are wind driven.

## Depth

Depth is a plain observed figure, Bonaire has no tidal range worth normalizing against. The reef itself runs through the recreational range, with the mooring in deep water at about 43 m requiring a swim to reach it.

## Hazards

* **Open water swim.** The mooring sits well offshore in deep water; the swim to and from the reef crosses open blue water.

## Wind

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dive area shore facing** | northeast, 42° |

* **Short fetch.** North-northwest through east (NNW-E), onshore over 1 to 4.7 km across the channel from Bonaire, enough to build a chop at the mooring. A strong trade from this quarter raises chop over the open swim to the mooring.
* **Mixed.** West-northwest through northwest (WNW-NW), cross-shore over 8.4 km to more than 30 km, running a swell along the reef.
* **Fine.** East-southeast clockwise through west: offshore from south-southeast through west, off the islet, and cross-shore over 1.8 to 3.1 km from the east-southeast and southeast.

## Visibility

Klein Bonaire's visibility runs excellent, commonly reaching up to 40 m.

## Temperature

* **At depth.** 26 to 29 C year round, coolest around February and March, warmest around August through October. No thermocline worth planning around at recreational depths.
* **Surface layer.** No sharp difference from depth here.

## Marine life

* **Reef.** Giant orange elephant ear sponges, often topped with black crinoids, and huge lavender stovepipe sponge colonies. Reef fish abound throughout.
