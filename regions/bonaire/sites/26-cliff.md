# 26. Cliff

One of Bonaire's better wall dives, in front of the Hamlet Oasis Villas just north of Captain Don's Habitat, combining a shallow reef plateau with a steep vertical wall running from about 9 to 22 m, covered in whip coral. A small partial cliff feature lies directly under the mooring buoy. The site also holds Captain Don's underwater Stone Memorial to the divers who have gone before, marked with a plaque and a dive flag.

## Getting there

* **Entry.** A shore entry is possible for guests of the Hamlet Oasis complex; Captain Don's Habitat, about 1 km south, runs a daily boat service to the site for other divers.
* **Parking.** At the Hamlet Oasis complex for the shore entry.
* **Access.** Open.
* **Facilities.** The Hamlet Oasis resort sits onshore; Dive Friends operates as the local dive centre.

## Navigation and landmarks

* **Captain Don's Stone Memorial.** Underwater, marked with a plaque and a dive flag, dedicated to the divers who have gone before.
* **The small cliff.** A partial cliff feature directly under the mooring buoy.

## Current

No tidal current here; Bonaire's currents are wind driven. Current here can run more challenging than the sites immediately south of it, part of why the wall suits an experienced diver.

## Depth

Depth is a plain observed figure, Bonaire has no tidal range worth normalizing against. The reef plateau leads to a vertical wall running from about 9 to 22 m.

## Hazards

* **Current and depth on the wall.** Suits an experienced diver rather than a beginner.

## Wind

| | |
|---|---|
| **Entry shore facing** | southwest, 236° |
| **Dive area shore facing** | west-southwest, 237° |

The swim out runs 92 m south-southeast from the entry, along the shore, to the dive area.

* **Bad.** South (S), onshore over 5.7 km down the coast, and west through west-northwest (W-WNW), onshore over more than 30 km of open water. Any wind from these quarters raises swell at the entry.
* **Short fetch.** South-southwest through west-southwest (SSW-WSW), onshore over 1.3 to 1.6 km from Klein Bonaire, enough to build a chop at the entry.
* **Mixed.** Northwest (NW), cross-shore over 6.2 km, running a swell along the wall past the entry.
* **Fine.** North-northwest clockwise through south-southeast: offshore from north through east-southeast, off the land, and cross-shore over 120 to 800 m from the north-northwest, southeast and south-southeast.

## Visibility

West coast visibility here is typically excellent, in the same 15 to 30 m range as the rest of the leeward coast.

## Temperature

* **At depth.** 26 to 29 C year round, coolest around February and March, warmest around August through October. No thermocline worth planning around at recreational depths.
* **Surface layer.** No sharp difference from depth here.

## Marine life

* **Wall.** Dense whip coral cover from 9 to 22 m.
