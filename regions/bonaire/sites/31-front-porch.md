# 31. Front Porch

An easy dive at the old Sunset Beach Resort, entered from shore just north of the property next to Den Laman Apartments, or from the beach at Eden Beach, home of the well known underwater webcam. A tugboat wreck lies off the old pier in the site's deep zone, its darkened recesses holding schools of big tooth cardinalfish rarely seen elsewhere on the island. The site's shallow sand terraces give way through a middle zone of hard coral and sponge to a deep zone of sand flats and garden eels around the wreck.

## Getting there

* **Entry.** Off shore just north of the old Sunset Beach Resort property next to Den Laman Apartments, or from Eden Beach.
* **Parking.** Roadside at either entry point.
* **Access.** Open.
* **Facilities.** Eden Beach and Den Laman Apartments sit onshore.

## Navigation and landmarks

* **Steel pipe.** Stands above water in front of Eden Beach, a landmark for the site.
* **The tugboat wreck.** Lies off the old pier at Sunset in the site's deep zone.
* **Depth profile.** Sand terraces with scattered coral run through the shallows, a middle zone of hard coral and sponge follows through the recreational range, and a deep zone of sand flats and garden eels holds the tugboat wreck.

* **Drivers.** The east to southeast trade winds. The current can sit still for days, then run hard when the trades pick up or shift.

## Wind

| | |
|---|---|
| **Entry shore facing** | west-northwest, 300° |
| **Dive area shore facing** | west-northwest, 300° |

* **Bad.** West-northwest through northwest (WNW-NW), onshore over 8 km to more than 30 km of open water. Any wind from this quarter raises swell at the entry.
* **Short fetch.** West (W), onshore over 1 km from Klein Bonaire, and north-northwest (NNW), onshore over 3.8 km from the coast to the north, each enough to build a chop at the entry.
* **Mixed.** South-southwest (SSW), cross-shore over more than 30 km, through the open gap south of Klein Bonaire, running a swell along the shore past the entry.
* **Fine.** North clockwise through south, and southwest through west-southwest: offshore from east-northeast through south, off the land, cross-shore over 70 to 860 m from the north-northeast, northeast and southwest, and onshore over 560 to 820 m from the north and west-southwest.

## Visibility

* **Overall.** Typically excellent, 15 to 30 m.

## Temperature

* **At depth.** 26 to 29 C year round, coolest around February and March, warmest around August through October. No thermocline worth planning around at recreational depths. A wetsuit or dive skin is standard.

## Marine life

* **The wreck.** Schools of big tooth cardinalfish, seldom seen elsewhere on the island.
* **Reef.** Trumpetfish, trunkfish, scorpionfish, schooling horse-eye jacks, soapfish, large grouper and moray eels.
* **Deep sand flats.** Garden eels around the wreck.

## Hazards

* **Current.** Wind driven current builds when the trades strengthen; check the surface before entering.
