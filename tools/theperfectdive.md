# The Perfect Dive (archived PNW site catalog)

<https://web.archive.org/web/20220413053905/http://theperfectdive.com/DEF-SiteList.asp>

A defunct catalog of Pacific Northwest dive sites (Puget Sound north and south, Hood Canal, the San Juans, the Strait of Juan de Fuca, and the Washington and Oregon coasts), each with a structured detail page (dive type, difficulty, entry, attractions) plus marine-life photo-ID galleries and a critter-sightings calendar.

- **Use it for.** A fast structured read on a site we don't know (difficulty, entry, what's down there) and to cross-check other community write-ups and print guides. It lists some sites the others skip.
- **Defunct, Wayback only.** Every read is a snapshot from around 2022, frozen in time and older than the active forums. Verify anything time-sensitive (access, fees, closures) against a recent source.
- **`web.archive.org` refuses the fetch tool.** `curl --compressed` the snapshot URL above instead. Prefer that pinned URL over re-querying `https://archive.org/wayback/available` for the "closest" snapshot; the closest capture on record can be a dead redirect stub with no real content, where the older pinned snapshot is known good.
- **Same rule as any community source.** Behaviour, not numbers. Re-derive depth and slack against NOAA CO-OPS and normalize depth to datum.
